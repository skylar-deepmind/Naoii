## About Naoii

Naoii is a multilingual community for training authentic expression. It helps language learners write more naturally — not just grammatically correct, but appropriate in tone, context, and nuance.

Whether you're composing a short moment or a longer article, you can post it and receive corrections from the community. Corrections come with explanations and tone notes, so you understand not just what to change, but why.

## Design Philosophy

Real-world expression matters more than textbook correctness. Naoii is built around three ideas:

- **Authentic expression** — practice with sentences you actually want to use
- **Community feedback** — learn from native and advanced speakers who explain context, not just grammar
- **Personal accumulation** — save adopted corrections to your library and review them over time

We emphasize high-quality corrections over volume. Adopting a correction earns the corrector reputation, incentivizing thoughtful feedback.

## Core Features

- **Moments & Articles** — two content types for short expressions and long-form writing
- **Correction system** — word-level diff visualization with explanations and tone annotations
- **Acceptance & reputation** — accept the best correction and award +10 reputation to the corrector
- **Expression library** — save adopted corrections for spaced-repetition review with private tags
- **Notifications** — real-time updates on corrections, acceptances, and reputation changes
- **Reporting & moderation** — flag inappropriate content with admin dashboard and analytics
- **Topics & events** — join community challenges and themed discussions
- **3-language UI** — Chinese, English, Japanese interface
- **Dark mode**

## How It Works

1. **Write** an expression in the language you're learning
2. **Get corrections** from the community — with diffs, explanations, and tone notes
3. **Accept** the most helpful correction to award reputation
4. **Save** it to your personal library and review later
